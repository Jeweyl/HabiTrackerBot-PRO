How it works

From first message to a week of data in three steps.

1

Start the bot

Open @HabiTrakerBot and send /start. Pick language and timezone when ready.

2

Build a real schedule

Add habits manually or with AI. Tasks appear on the calendar; conflicts surface before you commit.

3

Check in & review

Complete items in chat, answer check-ins, then open the dashboard when you want charts — premium features use points.
